About EXPECTO

Expecto is IIT Mandi's annual techfest — student-run, set in the Himalayas, built to make cutting-edge tech accessible to every college in India.

Our Story

Started in a single lecture hall in 2013 with 200 participants, Expecto now hosts 5000+ students from 150+ colleges. The name comes from Expecto Patronum — we summon the brightest ideas to fight the darkest problems.

EXPECTO-27 (the 27th edition numbering across IIT Mandi fests) focuses on AI for Himalayas: sustainability, disaster-tech, and mountain connectivity.
